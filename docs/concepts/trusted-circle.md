# Trusted circle

The trusted circle is the set of people who may release or block a customer's held transfers. The customer chooses them at enrolment, before any call, and the release contract records them.

## Membership is timelocked

A person added after enrolment becomes effective **72 hours** after the proposal. During those 72 hours, any effective member can veto the addition from their own phone. A scammer who coaches the customer into adding the scammer's "colleague" therefore gains nothing during the call, and the family sees the proposal and can stop it.

The same rule covers removals. When a member removes another member, the removal is immediate. When the customer removes a member, it waits for the timelock too, so a coached customer cannot empty the circle mid-call.

## The customer is never in their own circle

The contract refuses any release signed by the account holder, whatever their key or device. This is the core of the design: the person on the call cannot complete the payment the caller is asking for.

## What a member sees

A member's phone shows each live hold with:

- the payee, the amount and how far it is above the customer's usual payment;
- the call signals at the moment of sending: call length, screen sharing, a remote-control app;
- the customer's interview answers;
- the shared record, with the hash chain the contract holds.

The recommended action is to call the customer back on a known number, then decide.
